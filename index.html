    Delivery, cancellation and returns

    We deliver within until daily for . Delivery outside is agreed on WhatsApp before we dispatch. Delivery times are estimates and are confirmed in the chat. Orders placed after the delivery cut-off are delivered the next day we deliver, or can be collected from the shop during opening hours.

    You can cancel an order on WhatsApp before it is dispatched. Because these are alcoholic drinks, we cannot take back opened or used products. If an item arrives damaged, sealed incorrectly or is not what you ordered, tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we will replace it or refund you.

    Age and responsible drinking

    We only sell alcohol to adults aged and over. The age prompt on this site is not a substitute for checking: we may ask for a valid ID on delivery or collection and will refuse the sale if we cannot confirm your age. We do not sell to anyone who appears intoxicated. Please drink responsibly and never drink and drive.

    Privacy

    We collect only what we need to fulfil your order: your WhatsApp number, name (if you give it), delivery location and the items you order. We use it to confirm, deliver and support your order, and we do not sell it. Your cart, delivery choice and age confirmation are stored on your own device (in your browser) so they are still there when you return; clear your browser data to remove them. This site does not use advertising trackers. Messages sent on WhatsApp are also handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. We handle personal data in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act. To ask us to access, correct or delete your data, message us on WhatsApp.
